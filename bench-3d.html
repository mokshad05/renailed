ReNailed Model L

6-ft backless public bench: reclaimed old-growth fir slats bolted to a steel frame with a support every 24 in. Sized for a park path or plaza.[1][11]

Design basis

Slats: clean, unpainted, untreated, unglued wood[8], no paint from pre-1960 homes[12], ≤ 19% moisture[6], nail holes graded as knots[7]. Load check [C]: 1,500 N mid-slat on a 24 in span gives ≈ 5.2 MPa and ≈ 0.5 mm sag using Douglas-fir strength and stiffness[5], with salvaged wood ≈ 25% weaker[4]. This exceeds the 250 lb (1,112 N) ADA bench requirement[1]. Douglas-fir is moderately decay-resistant[5], so slats are replaceable. Park installation: surface-mount plates on a concrete pad or in-ground footings [T]. Comparable installed park bench: $5,000 in Burnaby[11]. US codes used as guides only; an engineer must review and load-test before public use.
