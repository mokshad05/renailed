ReNailed

Pull the nails. Keep the wood. Build it again. We turn clean, short old-growth wood from Vancouver teardowns into bolted public benches, tagged with the house the wood came from.

How it works

1 · Sort & SupplyPre-demolition survey; clean, unpainted, untreated wood only; contractor gets receipts for the $15,310 deposit file.
2 · De-Nail LabMetal-scan, de-nail, grade (nail holes as knots, USDA method), mill. Every board timed.
3 · Build & tagFive full-size slats bolted to a steel frame with a support every 24 in. No glue.
4 · MaintainWorn slats swap out; take-back at end of life; clean-wood recycling.
